// CITY OF DECAY

CITY OF DECAY:
REBORN 2

THE NIGHTMARE RETURNS

The nightmare returns.

Welcome back to the abandoned city, a place filled with secrets, forgotten experiments, and terrifying entities waiting in the darkness. Your mission is simple: explore the ruined streets, search for keys, unlock sealed areas, and find a way to reach the subway. But escaping won't be easy. Something is watching you.

As you explore the city, you will discover hidden secrets, mysterious notes, and clues that reveal more about the events that destroyed this place. Every locked door could hide something important, and every corridor could bring you closer to the truth — or into danger.

Be careful. Dudge is still out there, and the darkness is not as empty as it seems. Keep your flashlight ready, pay attention to your surroundings, and never assume you're alone. Other entities may be closer than you think.

With new challenges, terrifying encounters, and secrets waiting to be discovered, CITY OF DECAY: REBORN 2 takes you deeper into the mysterious world of City Of Decay.

Will you find the way out, or will the city become your final destination?

The nightmare is far from over.

Developed by ImadGamer14.

// CITY OF DECAY

CITY OF DECAY:
REBORN 3

THE CAVES

THE CITY WAS JUST THE BEGINNING...

Far beneath the abandoned city lies a forgotten underground world. A place where darkness never ends, where strange experiments were abandoned, and where something has been waiting for years.

You thought you had escaped the nightmare. You were wrong.

Explore enormous underground caves, discover terrifying secrets, uncover classified experiments and face creatures that were never supposed to exist.

But be careful... YOU ARE NOT ALONE DOWN THERE.

Every tunnel hides a mystery. Every sound could be your last warning. And deep inside the caves, something far more dangerous than anything you've faced before is waiting.

Can you survive the darkness? Can you uncover the truth?

THE DEEPER YOU GO, THE WORSE IT GETS.

THE NIGHTMARE CONTINUES.
